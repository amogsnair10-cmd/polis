Light Bulb: privacy policy

Last updated: 15 November 2026

Light Bulb is a study app. This page explains what it stores and why.

What stays on your phone

Your lesson progress, quiz scores, streak, review list, reminder time and any AI key you paste in for "Ask a doubt" are saved on your device. If you never sign in, none of this leaves your phone.

If you sign in with Google

We store your name, email address, study progress and the doubts you ask, in Google Firebase, so they follow you across devices. We use this only to run the app. We do not sell it or use it for advertising.

Ask a doubt

When you ask a doubt, your question and the lesson text it refers to are sent to the AI service you chose (Google Gemini or Anthropic Claude) using your own key, and are handled under that service's terms.

Reminders and the widget

Daily reminders are scheduled on your phone. The home-screen widget reads your streak from your phone. Neither sends data anywhere.

Photos

Lesson photos come from Wikimedia Commons under free licences, with credit shown on each image.

What we don't do

No ads, no tracking across other apps, no selling of data. The app does not collect your location, contacts or files.

Deleting your data

Sign out and clear the app's storage to remove everything on your phone. To delete your account data from our servers, write to the developer contact shown on the app's Google Play page and it will be removed.

Children

The app is meant for adult learners and exam candidates and is not directed at children under 13.
