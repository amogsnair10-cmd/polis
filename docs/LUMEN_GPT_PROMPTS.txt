LUMEN: PROMPTS FOR GPT IMAGE GENERATION
Light Bulb app mascot. 10 character sheets x 6 poses = 60 poses, plus 3 bonus single images.
======================================================================

HOW TO USE (works on a phone)
1. Open ChatGPT and start a NEW chat with image generation on.
2. In your FIRST message, attach your reference firefly picture and paste the
   'CHARACTER LOCK' below. Say: 'Remember this character. I will send prompts one by one.'
3. Then paste ONE sheet prompt at a time (each is complete). Ask for ONE image per message.
4. If Lumen starts to drift (different face, wings or bulb), re-attach the reference and add:
   'Match the reference exactly. Same face, same bulb, same wings.'
5. If a sheet has the wrong number of poses or any text in it, reply: 'Redo with exactly 6 poses in a 3x2 grid and no text.'
6. Save every sheet as PNG and send them to Claude in the chat. Claude will cut out each pose,
   remove the background, make small transparent files and put them in the app (quiz results,
   streak, badges, empty screens, loading screen and the phone widget).
7. Tip: ask for 2 versions of any sheet you like less, then pick the better one.

======================================================================
Lumen is the friendly mascot of a UPSC study app called Light Bulb. Lumen is a small firefly. Round, gentle head with two big simple dot eyes with tiny highlights, small curved eyebrows and a soft small smile. Slim little body, two small expressive hands, two small feet, two thin antennae with tiny gold tips, and delicate see-through wings with fine vein lines. Lumen's rounded abdomen is a glowing light bulb: a little glass bulb with a tiny filament inside and a warm gold glow. The personality is calm, kind, curious and dignified (a quiet study companion, never loud, goofy or babyish). Match the attached reference firefly exactly for face, proportions, wing shape and bulb design.
STYLE: refined hand-drawn ink illustration. Confident deep-navy line art with consistent line weight, flat colour fills, and a soft gold glow coming only from the bulb. Limited palette: deep navy (#0C1C36), warm gold (#F2C46D), parchment cream (#F3F1EC), with a very light teal accent only if needed. Clean, flat, vector-like. It should look at home next to elegant hand-drawn doodles.
