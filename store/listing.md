- **Full description (1498/4000):**

Light Bulb turns the UPSC syllabus into short, beautiful daily lessons that you can actually finish.

One lesson a day. Swipe through clear cards, see the photos and diagrams, then lock it in with a 10-question quiz.

WHAT YOU GET
• 11 subjects: PSIR (Political Science & International Relations), GS History, GS Geography, GS Environment, World History, Communist Theory, Astronomy, Physics, Chemistry, Biology and Quantum Mechanics
• Around 600 lessons, each with key cards, real photographs and hand-drawn doodles
• A 10-question quiz after every lesson, with a short explanation for every answer
• Chapter tests and mixed revision tests across everything you have learned
• Smart review: questions you miss come back after 1, 3, 7 and 21 days, until they stick
• Listen mode: have any lesson read aloud while you walk or commute
• Search across all lessons, and ask a doubt to an AI tutor (bring your own Gemini or Claude key)
• Streaks, XP, levels and badges to keep you coming back
• Daily reminders and a home-screen widget that shows your streak
• Sign in with Google to keep your progress safe across phones

BUILT FOR SERIOUS LEARNERS
Lessons are written to be exam-ready: precise names, dates and arguments, with an "Exam lens" card on how questions are usually asked.

Light Bulb is an independent study app. It is not affiliated with, endorsed by or connected to the Union Public Service Commission or any government body.

## App content declarations (answers)
- **App access:** all functionality is available without special access. Sign-in is optional.
- **Ads:** No ads.
- **Target audience:** 18 and over only.
- **Content rating questionnaire:** category "Reference, News or Educational". Answer every question truthfully. The history lessons describe wars and atrocities in a factual, educational way; if a question asks about violence, answer for that educational context and accept whatever rating results.
- **News app:** No. **Government app:** No (the app says it is independent). **Health/COVID:** No. **Financial features:** No.
- **Data safety:**
  - Collects personal info: name and email address (only if the user signs in with Google). Purpose: account management and app functionality. Not shared with third parties. Optional.
  - Collects app activity / user content: study progress and questions the user asks the AI tutor (only when signed in). Purpose: app functionality. Not shared. Optional.
  - Data encrypted in transit: Yes.
  - Users can request data deletion: Yes. In-app (Me -> Delete my account and data) and web: https://lightbulb.amogbuilds.net/privacy.html#delete
  - No data is sold. No advertising or analytics SDKs.
- **Account deletion URL (required because the app has sign-in):** https://lightbulb.amogbuilds.net/privacy.html#delete
